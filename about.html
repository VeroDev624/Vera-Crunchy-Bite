ABOUT VERA CRUNCHY BITE

Good snack brings people together.

Vera Crunchy Bites started with a simple craving in my own kitchen: a snack with an unmistakable,genuine crunch. one golden batch led to another.

What began as a small neighbourhood eatery has grown into a place where families, friends, students and professionals come together to enjoy great snacks.

From our kitchen to your table, we put care into every plate we serve.
